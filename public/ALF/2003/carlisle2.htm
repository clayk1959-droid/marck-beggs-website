Wendy Taylor Carlisle

After Life
            Argument Over Afterlife Leaves Man Dead Weekly World News

Right and wrong concurrently,
that’s Neils Bohr’s Theory of
Complementarity—
light both wave and particle,
the just description, and yet
contradictory.
Postulate your paradise:
with double jointed houris,
that long conversation with Nietzsche
or Freud. Think
Mozart on the clavier,
Dante over a long dinner. Be eager
to fight for your doctrine. Light,
your conviction; death, the necessary noun.
