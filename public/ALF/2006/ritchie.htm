Blog with a View

            After Terry Wright's Fractal Blog Page

1.
By the image in the word
Meaning casts its shadow 

By the word in the image
Colors strain to stain. 

2.
Flow sweeps away on
Mercury wings 

With magnification
The hidden emerges 

3.
Gaudy or polite
Or impish wisdom 

Sharing the void
Ought not be avoided 

4.
Unscroll the velour
From the vellum 

Gothics molt
In wild abandon

5.
Flint may spark
Or sandpaper erode 

But steel rings
Torque around the throat 

6.
Such a tease
Intuition 

All knotted up by
String theory 

7.
With each stroke
The enemy becomes cause 

Doubt has claws
Its lips bruise 

8.
Any time we stop the surge
To admire the frieze 

There is no we
Needles have eyes 

9.
Greening the blues
Gnawing white birch 

Thousand-year-old
Eggs evanesce 

10.
Mystery
Each miracle 

Accident
Each surprise

11.
Microscopic
Nannos are becoming 

Minarets
Under glass 

12.
What keeps the edges sharp
A mind at play 

Naming without guilt
Nothing but art
