Christy Ward

Vivacé

Scene 10

(Lights up on SL.  Sub Ann enters and discovers an apple on one of her flowers.)

 SUB ANN: Hey look, some forbidden fruit. (Picks it.) How about that. (Sings a little ditty.) Forbidden fruit. Forbidden fruit. I got me some forbidden fruit. (She takes a bite.) Mmm. Good. Maggots! I'm kidding. (She takes another bite.) Mm,Mm,Mm. Ugh! It's poison! I'm dying!  I'm kidding. (She takes yet another bite. She begins choking.) I'm kidding.  It's good! (She eats the apple and throws away the core.)

(Black out)
