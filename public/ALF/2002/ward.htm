Christy Ward

Vivacé

Characters

Ann:  A woman in her 20's.
Sub Ann:  Ann's subconscious. She usually functions tangentially but independently of Ann.
Scott:  Ann's boyfriend, in his 20's, a musician.

Setting

The stage is divided in half. Stage right is Sub Ann's domain. It is raised. There is a chair and three giant flowers. The flowers are made of many objects and Sub Ann continues building them throughout the play. Stage left has a couch and chair.

In the original production a film loop was made. The show began with it, and segments were shown throughout to highlight tone changes. It is up to the director what to include in the film or whether to even use one. The original cues will be noted.
