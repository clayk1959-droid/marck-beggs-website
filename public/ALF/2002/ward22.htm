Christy Ward

Vivacé

Scene 22

(Lights up. The stage is dim. Ann and Scott are snorting coke.)

SUB ANN: I want you to stop this.  This can destroy you. We've seen it before. Think back.  Come on, how pretty can you be with white chunks in you nose hairs?  But what a blast to feel so good.  To feel so charming.  You've got the world by the balls and you're gonna tell everyone in the room all about it. (Ann and Sub Ann snort a line at the same time. Then Ann and Scott begin making out.)  Oh yeah. This is great. I think I could fuck all night! (Pause.) Sounds pretty good though, huh? (Pause.) I want you to stop this. 

(Black out.)
