Jo McDougall

At Dusk

A woman stands behind a farmhouse 
in Kansas. 
Soothing her skirt, 
the woman leans into the wind. 
The wind takes some of the zinnias 
out of the pocket of the skirt.

A man walks toward her, 
coming in from the fields. 
Distance dances around them
 like sheets of dust.

 

 


G. Fisher
