Terry Morrison was born in Searcy, AR.  He lived in Arkansas as a child, mainly in Helena until he was 8 years old, when his family moved to Texas.  As an adult in his 30s he moved back to Arkansas, and lived on 40 acres on top of a mountain between Mountain View and Leslie.  He now lives in Austin, Texas where he tends his garden and is ignored by his cat, Collette.
