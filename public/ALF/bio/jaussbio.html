DAVID JAUSS teaches at UALR and in the low-residency MFA in Writing program at Vermont College. His most recent books are the poetry collections You Are Not Here (Fleur-de-Lis, 2002) and Improvising Rivers (Cleveland State University, 1995) and the short story collection Black Maps (University of Massachusetts, 1996).  You can find out more about Jauss at his website:  www.davidjauss.com.
