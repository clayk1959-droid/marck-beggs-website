Shannon L. Johnson lives in Little Rock, Arkansas, where she enjoys hiking, biking, writing, and reading Harry Potter books. And now added to the fun is a four-month old daughter, Lola Tess. Lola attended her first
women’s professional soccer game when she was two months old.

Shannon began teaching writing at the University of Central Arkansas after she earned a Master of Liberal Arts degree from Henderson State University in 1999. She likes to write stories for kids and grown-ups.
