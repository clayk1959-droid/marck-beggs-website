Andra Buschmann received her BFA in Theatre Studies with a double emphasis in Playwrighting and Directing from Southern Methodist University in May 2001.  A native of Batesville, she resides in Dallas, Texas where she is pursuing a career in acting.  Currently, Andra is starring in the Pocket Sandwich Theatre's production of Psycho Beach Party.    Her play, Coon Dogs, Lesbians, and Love was produced by SMU's Buffet Company in 2000.
