Erin O’Neill has published poetry and fiction in Mississippi Review, Mankato Poetry Review, and the Louisiana English Journal.  Her short story “The Yellow Bird” was recently made into a radio play.  Recently relocated from New Mexico, she is currently employed as Associate Professor of English at the University of Arkansas—Monticello, where she teaches technical writing and composition.
