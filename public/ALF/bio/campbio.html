Marcia Camp lives in Little Rock.  Her essays, profiles, and features have been published regionally and nationally.  Marcia's short stories and poetry have appeared in literary journals, and she has twice won the Sybil Nash Abrams Poetry Award.  She is the author of You Can't Leave Till You Do the Paperwork:  Matters of Life and Death, a self-help book. "Subjects that capture my interest sometimes need the breadth of prose while others require the penetrating depth of poetry."
