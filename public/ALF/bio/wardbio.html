Christy Ward is a graduate of the University of Arkansas in Fayetteville. She's written and acted in a number of strange plays. You may remember seeing her outrageously beautiful face on the silver screen in her role as Melinda in Sling Blade. She has recently returned to Little Rock, Arkansas after three years husband hunting in Amsterdam. She bagged one and has brought him home. She now works as an editor and writer for the Little Rock Free Press.
