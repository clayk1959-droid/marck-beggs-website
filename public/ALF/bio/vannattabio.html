DENNIS VANNATTA has published stories in many journals and anthologies, including Antioch Review, The Quarterly, and Pushcart XV. Two of his collections—This Time, This Place and Prayers For the Dead—have been published by White Pine Press.  Another collection, Lives of the Artists, is available from Livingston Press.  Vannatta teaches English at UALR.
