Kevin Brockmeier is the author of the story collection Things That Fall from the Sky and the children's novel City of Names. His novel The Truth About Celia, from which 'The Green Children" is taken, will be published by Pantheon in the summer of 2003. He is currently working on a second children's novel, Grooves; or, The True-Life Outbreak of Weirdness.
