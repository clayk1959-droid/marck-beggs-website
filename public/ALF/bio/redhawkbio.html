Red Hawk was the Hodder Fellow in the Humanities at Princeton University. He now teaches writing at University of Arkansas Monticello where he lives with his wife Chandrika. His recent book of poems is The Art of Dying (Hohm Press, 1999). Other books include: The Way of Power (Hohm Press, 1996), The Sioux Dog Dance (Cleveland State University, 1991), and Journey of the Medicine Man (August House, 1983).
