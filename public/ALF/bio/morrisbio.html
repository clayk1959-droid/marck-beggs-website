NICHOLAS B. MORRIS graduated from Arkansas Tech with a BFA in Creative Writing and in 2007 will earn an MFA in Writing & Poetics from the Jack Kerouac School of Disembodied Poetics in Boulder, CO. His poetry and prose have appeared in Nebo, The Rectangle, At Your Ear, Summer Stock, Guilty As Charged, and Cliterature. His chapbook, The Scent of Yellow. was published by AlTURNitively Press in 2005, and with Michelle Le. McKinney he co-directed Tolerate This, a documentary about poetry slams in Arkansas. He currently serves as Editor-in-Chief of Bombay Gin #33 and wishes Colorado had real fried chicken.
