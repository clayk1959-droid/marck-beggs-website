STEVEN CAMPBELL has worked as a writer and photographer for Downtowner, Nightclub & Bar and other magazines in the Mid-South. He has covered the trials and appellate hearings of two high-profile murder cases as a reporter for the Evening Times. Campbell has also been featured as an Arts and Entertainment writer for the Memphis Business Journal.

He has had his poetry and fiction published in a number of literary magazines and forums.
