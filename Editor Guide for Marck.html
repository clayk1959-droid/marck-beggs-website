How to Update Your Site

A short guide to adding and changing your music and writings — no coding, nothing to install.

1Log in

Go to marck-beggs-website.vercel.app/editor in any web browser — your phone, laptop, whatever's handy. Type in your email and the password Clay gave you, then click Log in.

What happens: Nothing changes on the site yet — this just gets you into the editing tools. You'll stay logged in for about a month, so you shouldn't need to do this often.

2Pick Music or Writings

You'll see a page with two things you can manage: Music (your albums and singles) and Writings (your books and anthologies). Click whichever one you want to change.

3Change something that's already there

Every album or book is listed with an Edit button and a Delete button next to it.

  • Edit opens up the fields for that one entry — title, year, links, and so on. Change whatever you want, then click Save.
  • Delete asks "are you sure?" first, then removes it completely.
What happens: After you click Save (or confirm Delete), you'll see a "✓ Saved" message right there for a couple of seconds — that's your confirmation it actually went through, and it tells you which one it saved, so you know it grabbed the right entry. The real site takes about a minute to catch up after that. If you check right away and it looks unchanged, that's normal — give it a minute and refresh the page.
One thing to watch for: Wait for that "✓ Saved" message to actually appear before you click Edit on the next thing. If you click too fast, right as one row is collapsing back down, you can end up clicking into the wrong entry by accident.

4Add something brand new

At the top of the Music or Writings page, click + Add a release or + Add a book. A blank form opens up.

  1. Choose a cover photo from your computer or phone — click "Choose File" and pick it, same as attaching a photo to an email.
  2. Fill in the title and whatever else applies (year, who it's by, etc.) — these can all be edited again later if you change your mind.
  3. For music only: paste in the link(s) to wherever it's streaming (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, SoundCloud, Pandora) — leave any blank you don't have.
  4. Click Add release or Add book.
What happens: Your photo uploads and gets automatically resized to fit the site — you don't need to resize anything yourself beforehand. You'll see a "✓ Added" confirmation, then it collapses back to the button. Same as editing, give it about a minute to show up on the actual site.
About the music links: If you only have one streaming link for a release, tapping its cover on the site takes people straight to that player — no extra clicking for them. If you have more than one, tapping the cover shows a little menu so they can pick Spotify, Apple Music, whichever they use.

If something looks wrong

You can't break anything permanently — every change can be undone. If you're not sure what happened, or something looks off after a change, just text or email Clay and he'll sort it out. Nothing here requires knowing any code.
